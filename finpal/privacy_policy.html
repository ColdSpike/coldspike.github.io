Privacy Policy for FinPal

Last updated: December 29, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how FinPal ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

A. SMS Data (Financial Transactions)

FinPal requests access to your SMS messages solely for the purpose of automatically tracking your income and expenses.

  • What we access: We scan your SMS inbox for transaction-related messages (e.g., from banks, credit cards, or mobile wallets).
  • How it is processed: All SMS processing happens locally on your device. We do not upload your raw SMS messages to any external server.
  • AI Processing: If you enable AI features (powered by Google Gemini), strictly anonymized transaction text may be processed to categorize expenses. No personally identifiable information (PII) is shared.

B. Personal Information

  • Account Info: When you sign in with Google, we collect your email address and display name to create your account and sync your premium status.
  • Financial Data: Your transaction history, budget, and categories are stored locally on your device.

C. Device Information

We may collect information about your mobile device, including model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (for crash reporting and purchase verification).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  1. Provide Features: Automatically generate transaction records from your bank SMS.
  2. Manage Accounts: Verify your identity and sync your Premium membership across devices.
  3. Improve App: Analyze crash logs (via Firebase Crashlytics) to fix bugs and improve performance.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication.
  • Google Play Services: For In-App Purchases and license verification.
  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database, and crash reporting.
